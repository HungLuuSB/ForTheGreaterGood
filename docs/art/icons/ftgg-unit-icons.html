Unit icon set, draft 5

Built on BAR's documented three-part rule, with our own frames and symbols. The frame shape is the unit type, the dots under it are the tech level (none for T1, two for T2, three for T3), and the white symbol inside is the weapon or role. Every icon is drawn in white with a dark edge, so one image takes any team color in Roblox through ImageColor3. Draft 5 covers every one of the game's 159 unit definitions (read 24 Oct 2026, 16:00), including the new T1 defenses from S-157: laser towers, the ramping Beamer and the lobbing guns. Retired units keep an icon for old replays.
