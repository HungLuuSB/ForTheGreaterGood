Order and Chaos emblems

Two directions, each a matched pair, in the unit icons' style B: a white shape with a hairline dark edge and a soft shadow, tinted in the faction colour (Order Blue, Chaos Red). Order stands for discipline, precision and control, heraldry and law, the fortress side; Chaos for freedom, force and disruption, beasts and ruin, mass assault. Neither copies a known mark: no eight-pointed star, no eagle, no claw marks, nothing from BAR.

The two directions

Each emblem at 16, 24, 32 and 64 px, then 160 px; faction colour on dark, white source, and on light

A, heraldic. Order's Bastion: a fortress crest with battlements, a rising chevron and a sighting aperture (control, precision, defence). Chaos's Maw: a horned beast's head, jagged brow and slit eyes, coming to a point (predator, force). B, abstract. Order's Lodestar: a four-pointed star held whole in a ring (a fixed point, discipline). Chaos's Fracture: a downward wedge shattered by a jagged crack, its shards forced apart (disruption, ruin).
