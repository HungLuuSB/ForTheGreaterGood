For The Greater Good · E-2 · strategic icons · round 2

Three icon directions

The same 12 units drawn three ways. All three keep the grammar players learn: the frame is the unit type, the dots are the tech level, the symbol is the weapon or role. All three share one new set of symbols, built from solid shapes rather than thin lines so they survive at strategic zoom. Round 2 drops the cartoon look of round 1: straight cuts and sharp tips instead of rounded blobs, faceted ship and sub hulls, hairline edges with a soft shadow instead of thick black outlines, and square tier pips. The directions differ in how the frame and symbol are painted. Every icon is still one white image with a dark edge, tinted per team in Roblox.

What changed from draft 6, in all three

Fixes from the E-1 audit

Ships

The ship frame is a faceted, pointed bow seen from above, so it can't be read as a structure. The submarine frame is now full size, a hull with a sail.

Fighter vs bomber

Fighters keep the anti-air chevron. Bombers drop two solid bombs, so they no longer mirror the chevron.

Destroyer

A gun over a torpedo: its real weapons (gun, ASW torpedoes). Draft 6 gave it the radar-SAM symbol.

Edges

Symbols are filled shapes, not thin lines. The dark edge is a hairline with a soft shadow under the icon, so icons sit on the ground like decals, not stickers.
