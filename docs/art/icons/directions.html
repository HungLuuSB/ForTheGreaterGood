For The Greater Good · E-2 · strategic icons

Three icon directions

The same 12 units drawn three ways. All three keep the grammar players learn: the frame is the unit type, the dots are the tech level, the symbol is the weapon or role. All three share one new set of symbols, built from solid shapes rather than thin lines so they survive at strategic zoom. The directions differ in how the frame and symbol are painted. Every icon is still one white image with a dark edge, tinted per team in Roblox.

Side by side at in-game size

Each row is one direction: Order, then Chaos, then the same icons on the map. The game's base icon width is 22 px (Icons.BASE); 24 px is the low end of what you'll see at strategic zoom.

What changed from draft 6, in all three

Fixes from the E-1 audit

Ships

The ship frame is a pointed bow seen from above, so it can't be read as a structure. The submarine frame is now full size, a hull with a sail.

Fighter vs bomber

Fighters keep the anti-air chevron. Bombers drop two solid bombs, so they no longer mirror the chevron.

Destroyer

A gun over a torpedo: its real weapons (gun, ASW torpedoes). Draft 6 gave it the radar-SAM symbol.

Line weight

No stroke thinner than 4 units of 48 (2 px at 24 px). Symbols are mostly filled shapes with a dark edge.
