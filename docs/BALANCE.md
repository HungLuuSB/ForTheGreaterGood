- Equal value per side: about 3000 (value = supply + power / 10), at most 20 units.
- Two starts: **close** (120 studs apart) and **long** (600 studs apart, both attack-move). 1 seed(s) each; at most 1800 ticks (120 s).
- Cell: Order's value trade, close / long = Chaos value destroyed ÷ Order value lost (damage counted pro rata). Above 1 favours Order, ∞ means Order lost nothing. Letters: winner per seed (O, C, or - when time ran out with both sides alive).
- Blank: neither unit can damage the other.
- Aircraft sides get their faction's Advanced Airfield at the map edge behind them (not part of the trade, durable) and a full account, so they rearm as in a match (D103).
- Bomber groups bring their faction's T1 radar tower (not part of the trade), as a match's base radar would, so bombs can target radar contacts (D96).

## Counter check (guide 12.4)

Each matrix cell is checked for every unit of that role on both sides, against every enemy unit of that target class. A pair falls short when:

- **—**: the attacker can damage the class anyway;
- **●**: it destroys nothing;
- **●●**: its value trade is below 1;
- **●●●**: its value trade is below 2.

Every cell needs the attacker to destroy something: a fight where nothing died on either side is not an infinite trade. **Same-role pairs** (a role against its own kind: tanks against tanks, destroyers against destroyers) are judged differently (D98): both sides must be able to damage each other, and the equal-cost trade should stay within the soft band 0.5–2.0. They are listed below the findings.

Against structures (the siege), the share of the base destroyed must be above 2% for ●, 30% for ●●, and 60% for ●●●. The coastal battery row comes from guide 13.5 ("heavy gun against ships": ●●●).

Why a pair fell short:

- **mechanic**: can't target, can't see, can't reach (the shore), out of range, never hit, a bomber never lined up a run, or all its bombs on radar contacts missed;
- **numbers**: it saw, reached, fired and hit, but lost the trade.
