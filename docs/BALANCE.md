- **—**: the attacker can damage the class anyway;
- **●**: it destroys nothing;
- **●●**: its value trade is below 1;

- **mechanic**: can't target, can't see, can't reach (the shore), out of range, never hit, ran dry, or a bomber never lined up a run;
