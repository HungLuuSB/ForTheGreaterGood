## Phase 3: new units

A writes the defs and decisions first, then tells B (icons), C (models) and D (Codex roles) every new ID.

- **AIR:** Chaos T2 "Dragon" (flying fortress: big, tough, fast; a fire gun onto the ground and 2 green lasers); Order T2 "Liche" (strategic bomber: devastating bomb, long reload, fast, slow turn); a T1 transport per faction (light units and structures; new load/unload orders).
- **BOT:** a new move class (steep slopes, fast, turns in place) and bot labs. Bot constructor (less build power, low health); scouts (Chaos: many plasma shots; Order: tougher, alternating arm lasers); Mace (Order, long-range plasma) / Thug (Chaos); Lazarus / Graverobber (repair, resurrect, reclaim; no building); an AA bot per faction; Centurion (Chaos, tough, long red laser against swarms); Fatboy (Order, slow heavy plasma bot, big area damage); Tzar (Chaos T2 vehicle: a bit less range than Fatboy, more damage, faster reload).
