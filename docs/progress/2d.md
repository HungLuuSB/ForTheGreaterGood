1. **C renders.** Each def's approved mesh (else its blockout), with portraits.py's camera, at 256 × 256 square. Two passes:
   - the picture, with the team paint in neutral grey;
   - a white mask of the team paint alone.
   One rendered angle for every unit; ships at the waterline, subs below it (as `PortraitStage`).
2. **E composites.** Each picture goes on a dark plate per domain, close to the tile's `#3A3E46` (ground, air, sea; no terrain detail, so the unit reads at 66 px). **Why:**
- A grid of 30–60 ViewportFrames costs more each frame than ImageLabels.
- Every picture shares one light and one angle.
- The lobby, loading screens and thumbnails can reuse them.

**Costs:**
- About 8 uploads (with the user's approval).
- A re-render whenever C changes a model: 203 × ~8 s ≈ 30 min in Blender.
- The pictures don't animate.

**Alternative (B):** keep the live 3D portraits. E would only draw the tile's frame and the domain backdrop plates, with no per-unit pictures.

Hand-painted 2D pictures of 203 units are not proposed: they would drift from C's models.

**Method:** I drew every draft-6 icon at 22 px (the game's `Icons.BASE`, times the tier scale) on the Supreme Strait minimap, in Order blue and Chaos red, plus the dark team colours Navy and Crimson.

**Coverage:** 203 defs (5 retired) map exactly once onto 106 icons, plus 10 placeholders. `check.mjs` finds no missing or extra ids.

Notes from the check, all deliberate:
- The shield domes use the defense frame without a weapon.
- The nuke silos use the structure frame with a weapon.
- Only the placeholder uses the hover frame; no hover unit exists yet.

### Reads well
- **Frames:** diamond (vehicle), circle (walker), triangle and inverted triangle (jet and helicopter), square (structure) and shield (defense) are clear at 22 px in both team colours. The dark fill behind the frame holds up on grass, rock and the pink rock.
- **Tier dots:** two and three dots read well.
- **Symbols:**
  - The Commander star, the hammer (builder) and the scout arrow.
  - The factory symbols, each the frame of what it builds.
  - The nuke trefoil and the extractor drill.
  - The anti-air chevron on ground and defense frames.
  - The question mark on the placeholders.

### Weak or too alike at 22 px
1. **Ship and structure frames.** The ship (a square with a rounded top) and the structure square are the same blob at 22 px. Corvette and Destroyer look like Vehicle Plant and Shipyard.
2. **Fighter and bomber.** The fighter (`aa`, chevron up) and the bomber (`bombs2`, chevron down) are one shape mirrored inside the same triangle. The same goes for air superiority (`aa2`) and the strategic bomber (`bombs3`). This is the most expensive confusion in a match.
3. **Decoy drone and strategic bomber.** The decoy is the strategic bomber's symbol, dashed. The dashes vanish below about 30 px, so your own Wraith reads as a bomber.
4. **Sensors.** Radar (dot and arcs), sonar (the same, rotated), tracking (crosshair) and pinpointer (pin) are thin-line symbols that merge at 22 px.
5. **Thin-line weapons.**
   - The laser bolts (`laser1/2/3`) and the ramp beam use 2.3-unit lines and disappear on rock.
   - Point defence (falling missile plus burst) and anti-nuke (ringed trefoil) are both busy round blobs.
   - Mortar and artillery differ only in the ball's size.
6. **Submarine frame.** It is short and its symbol is drawn at 0.82 scale, so the Silence and Lamprey are the smallest icons on the field.
7. **Economy stripes.** Storage (three bars), wall (bricks), rearm pad (H) and energy storage (cell) are all horizontal stripes in a square.
8. **Plasma and scout on the walker frame.** Plasma (ball in a ring) next to the scout arrow on a circle is fine at 48 px but close at 22 px.
9. **Line weight overall.** The frame stroke is 3.4 units of 48, about 1.5 px at 22 px. The frames survive because of their dark fill; the symbols with no fill do not.

### Defs whose icon gets the role wrong
- **Crusader and Corsair (destroyers)** carry a gun, ASW torpedoes, sonar and a naval SAM. The icon is `aa2`, the radar-SAM symbol, which also says "Pharos / Scylla". They are the navy's sub hunters and gun line, so they want a gun-and-torpedo symbol.
- **Dragon** (Chaos flying fortress) fires a flame stream and two lasers. The icon is the heavy-cannon shell. It wants a flame symbol (BAR's grammar has fire as its own weapon role).
- **Warden** "unpacks into a fortified emplacement" and can't fire while packed. It shares the Paladin and Goliath's Main Battle Tank icon, so a deployed Warden line reads as tanks. It wants a heavy gun with a deploy mark (legs or anchor).
- **Hellmouth** is a "self-propelled mortar" but uses the artillery arc. The Cyclops siege mortar uses the mortar symbol. Either the Hellmouth takes the mortar symbol at T1, or the two symbols get clearly different shapes (see weak point 5).
- **Minotaur** is a siege ram with a demolition charge, under Main Battle Tank. Acceptable, but it is the one T2 tank that can't fight units well. Worth its own symbol if one fits.
- **Commanders, Titan and Juggernaut (`chs_korgoth`)** are `AMPHIB_WALKER`. The walker circle is right by the frame rule, but nothing shows they cross water. Fine for now, since BAR's grammar gives amphibious bots their own frame and we have only these four.
- **Naming hazard (not an icon problem):** `chs_juggernaut` is named "Goliath" and `chs_korgoth` is named "Juggernaut". Tables that show ids next to names will confuse readers.

### What the new set must fix
- A ship frame that is unmistakably not a structure.
- Bombers drawn as solid bombs, not a mirrored chevron.
- Sensors as solid, distinct shapes.
- A minimum stroke of about 4 units (about 2 px at 22 px) or filled shapes.
- A full-size sub frame.
- Destroyer, Dragon and Warden symbols that match their weapons.
